## Annex A: CLA vs. DCO in Teros

To ensure maximum transparency with our developer community, we clarify our governance policy and the rationale for requiring a comprehensive CLA rather than a simple Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO).

- **DCO:** A lightweight mechanism consisting of a `Signed-off-by` tag in commits, attesting that the developer has the right to submit the code. However, it is not a contract, does not grant explicit patent licenses, and does not confer relicensing rights.
- **CLA (This Document):** A complete and binding legal contract that grants explicit, broad, and irrevocable licenses to Teros AI over the copyright and patents of the Contributions.

**Why Teros requires a CLA:**

- **Legal Security and Audits:** It provides an undeniable chain of title for intellectual property, essential for passing technical and legal Due Diligence.
- **Dual Licensing Enablement:** It allows Teros AI to offer Teros under a source-available or open-source license for the community, and simultaneously under commercial (Enterprise) licenses for large corporations, ensuring the project's financial sustainability.
- **Investment and M&A:** It protects the project's value in potential investment or acquisition scenarios.
- **Patent Defense:** The retaliation clause protects all Teros users against malicious patent litigation.
